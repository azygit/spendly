Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Spendly ("we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, and protects your information when you use our personal expense tracking application ("Service"). By using Spendly, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.

1. Data We Collect

To provide the Service, we collect the information you give us directly, including:

  • Account information, such as your name and email address
  • Expense records you enter, including amounts, categories, dates, and descriptions
  • Basic usage information, such as login timestamps, needed to keep your account secure

2. How We Use Your Data

We use the information we collect to operate and improve Spendly, including to:

  • Create and maintain your account
  • Display your expense history, categories, and spending summaries back to you
  • Communicate with you about your account or the Service
  • Detect, prevent, and address technical issues or misuse

We do not use your expense data to build advertising profiles, and we do not sell your personal data to third parties.

3. Data Storage

Your account and expense data are stored in our database and retained for as long as your account is active, or as needed to provide the Service to you. We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your data from unauthorized access, loss, or misuse. You may request deletion of your account and associated data at any time by contacting us.

4. Third Party Services

We may rely on trusted third-party providers for functions such as hosting, authentication, or email delivery. These providers only receive the information necessary to perform their function and are not permitted to use your data for any other purpose. Spendly does not share your expense data with advertisers or data brokers.

5. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or how your data is handled, please reach out to us through your account settings or the contact details provided on our website.
